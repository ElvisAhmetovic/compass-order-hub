# Rename email sender to Media Marketing LTD and point links to mediaamarketing.com/de

## What changes
1. **Sender name** on every email (offers, invoices, reminders, confirmations, portal, tickets, team notices): "AB Media Team" becomes **Media Marketing LTD**. The address stays noreply@mediaamarketing.com.
2. **Branding inside emails**: "AB Media Team" in headers, footers, signatures, subjects ("... | AB Media Team") and the WhatsApp greeting text becomes "Media Marketing LTD".
3. **Website links** in emails (footer "www.abm-team.com") now go to https://www.mediaamarketing.com/de and read "www.mediaamarketing.com".
4. **Same website elsewhere**: the default website on offer PDFs and invoice company settings, and the website link in the CRM sidebar, change to https://www.mediaamarketing.com/de. Company settings that were saved with the old abm-team.com address get the new one too.
5. Offers sent without a staff name show "Media Marketing LTD" instead of "AB Media Team".

## Not changing
- Email sending address (already mediaamarketing.com) and the empriatech.com links for client portal and offer confirmation.
- Recognition of old team addresses (team-abmedia.com etc.) for internal notifications.
- Inventory import labels and old internal banners in the CRM.
- Emails already sent.
