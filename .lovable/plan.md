# Use Invoice@mediaamarketing.com as the team inbox

## Answer for the boss
The test offer went to invoice@team-abmedia.com only because I picked that address for the test. But the CRM also sends its internal copies there: new orders, confirmed offers, payments, reminders, monthly packages, tickets and work-hour notices. Clients never see these copies.

## Change
- Replace invoice@team-abmedia.com with **invoice@mediaamarketing.com** everywhere the CRM sends internal copies (about 20 places). The old address stops getting them.
- Keep recognising old team-abmedia.com addresses as team members, so internal emails stay internal.
- Add mediaamarketing.com to the team domains as well.
- Redeploy all affected email functions, then send a test offer to invoice@mediaamarketing.com.

## Not changing
- Other team addresses in the lists (personal Gmail addresses, service@, Ikram@ and so on).
- The sender, which stays Media Marketing LTD <noreply@mediaamarketing.com>.
