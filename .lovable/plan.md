# Swap kleinabmedia@gmail.com for invoice@mediaamarketing.com in team emails

## Cause
The order status emails to the team (created, in progress, invoice sent, invoice paid, resolved, cancelled and so on) use the recipient list saved on the Notification Settings page. That saved list still has kleinabmedia@gmail.com and does not have invoice@mediaamarketing.com. In the last 3 days, 35 of these emails went to that list.

## Change
- In the saved Notification Settings recipients: remove **kleinabmedia@gmail.com** and add **invoice@mediaamarketing.com**. All other recipients stay the same.
- No other email list in the CRM includes kleinabmedia@gmail.com. invoice@mediaamarketing.com is already on the lists for new orders, invoices sent, payments, reminders, offers and tickets.

## Not changing
- kleinabmedia@gmail.com keeps its admin rights for work hours. That is a login permission, not an email list.

## After
- Check the saved list and show it on the Notification Settings page. It changes right away, no publish needed.
