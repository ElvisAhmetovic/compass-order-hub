# Finalise standard privacy, jurisdiction, payment, and support clauses

## Privacy policy
- Replace the “data protection officer coming soon” placeholder in German and English with a clear statement that no data protection officer has been appointed because the company currently understands that it is not legally required to appoint one.
- Direct all privacy enquiries and data-subject requests to `kontakt@empriatech.com` and the published company contact details.
- Keep the existing GDPR rights, controller information, processing purposes, retention, security, and complaint rights.
- Keep unknown hosting-provider and third-party-service details marked as coming soon; do not invent providers or tools.
- Phrase the non-appointment statement as the company’s current determination, not as legal advice or an unconditional claim that can never change.

## Governing law and B2B jurisdiction
- Replace the placeholder in the bilingual Terms with **the laws of England and Wales**.
- State that the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction for B2B disputes, subject to any mandatory law or individually agreed contract term.
- Keep the Terms limited to business clients.

## Payment terms
- State that invoices are issued after the agreed services are provided, unless an individual proposal or contract defines milestones or another schedule.
- Require payment by bank transfer within **14 calendar days** of the invoice date, without deduction.
- Keep bank-account details on the invoice rather than publishing them on the website.
- Preserve statutory rights concerning late payment and allow individually agreed payment terms to take priority.

## Support rules
- State that general support requests are handled Monday–Friday during normal business hours, excluding public holidays.
- Make clear there is no guaranteed response or resolution time unless a proposal, maintenance plan, or service agreement specifies one.
- Limit included support to the agreed service scope; additional work, new features, third-party failures, and client-caused issues may be quoted separately.
- Keep emergency, uptime, and service-level commitments contract-specific rather than inventing guarantees.

## Consistency and verification
- Update both German and English versions in the shared legal-page content so Privacy and Terms stay aligned.
- Keep CRM proposals, invoices, PDFs, internal payment settings, and the active bank-account instructions unchanged.
- Record the confirmed B2B rules as public-site business requirements for future legal-page updates.
- Verify Privacy and Terms in both languages, confirm placeholders were replaced only where information is now known, and check the public site builds cleanly.

## Legal note
These are general B2B clauses based on the owner’s confirmed choices. A qualified lawyer should review them before they are relied upon as binding contractual terms.
