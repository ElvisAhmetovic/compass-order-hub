# Architecture rules

- Company money totals (dashboard finance cards, dashboard status-card € sums, invoice Outstanding/Paid, monthly Revenue) render only when `canViewFinanceTotals(user.email)` from `src/config/financeAccess.ts` passes — why: owner-only privacy via a fixed email allowlist, so new accounts never see them.
- Keep the approved English/German payment-reminder wording in `src/services/paymentReminderCopy.ts` for client-side editors; automatic invoice reminders and server fallback must mirror it — why: all client payment-reminder channels should deliver the same requested message.
- Keep active payment-account instructions consistent across browser PDFs, offer PDFs, monthly PDFs, and reminder functions — why: customers must never receive conflicting bank details from different sending paths.
- Keep the root route as the public Empria homepage for every visitor, including authenticated users — why: opening empriatech.com must never redirect away from the company homepage.
- Keep public marketing routes and shared public-site navigation separate from authenticated CRM and client areas — why: service pages must remain indexable and accessible without a session.
- Keep public legal content in the shared bilingual legal-page system and link every public legal page from the footer — why: company disclosures must stay consistent across the marketing site.
- Keep the landing page and `/kontakt` on the shared public contact-form component and validation schema — why: both inquiry entry points must submit and validate identically.

- AI win-back offers: drafts are created by `outreach-scan` and only sent by `outreach-send` after a team member approves; rules live in `supabase/functions/_shared/outreachRules.ts` — why: no AI email reaches a client without human review.
- AI outreach drafts read full client context (orders incl. internal notes, invoices, packages) via `supabase/functions/_shared/outreachContext.ts`, skip clients with unpaid invoices, and suggest only services from `service_catalog`/`service_insights` — why: offers must be grounded and never reach clients who owe money.

- Follow `PROJECT_RULES.md` (pre-build spec, tests, verification gates, read-only live data) for every change — why: team quality rules adapted to this project.
- Stripe subscriptions for monthly packages: all Stripe calls go through `stripe-billing` (team/client actions, ownership checked server-side) and status/payments change only in the signature-verified, idempotent `stripe-webhook`; `generate-monthly-installments` skips contracts Stripe bills (`stripeBillsContract`) — why: no double billing and no client-trusted payment state.
- New orders, monthly packages and accepted offers call `ensure-client-portal`, which creates a client portal account only when none exists and never resets an existing password — why: every client can reach their orders and payments without a manual step.
- One-time Stripe payment links on orders live in `order_payment_links`; they are created/sent only by team via `stripe-billing` (create_order_link/send_order_link), a new link deactivates the old one, and only the verified `stripe-webhook` marks them paid — why: clients can never pay a stale amount or fake a payment.
- Portal order ownership is one rule: linked account, company owner, or matching contact email — enforced by `public.client_owns_order` in RLS, mirrored by `src/services/clientOwnership.ts` and the `stripe-billing` overview — why: an order must be visible the same way on every portal page.
- Portal data loaders must surface load failures (retry card / `must()` on the server), never turn errors into empty lists — why: clients must never be shown "nothing owed" when billing failed to load.
- Client portal actions are written to `client_portal_activity` via `src/services/portalActivity.ts` (fire-and-forget, clients insert-only, admins/agents read) and mirrored as `project.portal_*` analytics events inline — why: the team needs a dated per-client log, analytics only gives anonymous totals.
- Admin assistant: `admin-assistant` only reads via whitelisted tables in `_shared/assistantRules.ts` and only *proposes* changes into `assistant_actions`; `admin-assistant-confirm` performs them after an admin clicks Confirm — why: no AI-made change reaches live data without a human click.
- Assistant composite reads and reviewed batches live in `_shared/assistantOperations.ts`; confirmation claims actions atomically, rechecks recipients/status/opt-outs and records per-item outcomes — why: multi-record requests must not hide failures or repeat side effects.
- Invoice reminder email rendering is shared in `_shared/invoiceReminderEmail.ts` by automatic reminders and assistant-approved reminders — why: both sending paths must use identical approved wording and bank instructions.
- Team access is admin/agent only: RLS uses `public.is_team()` (never `NOT is_client()`), and the app gates CRM pages/menus with `isTeamRole` in `src/components/auth/teamAccess.ts`; there is no public sign-up, team accounts come only from `create-user` — why: self-made accounts must never see team data, AI or Team Chat.
- AI outreach replies: offers use a per-offer reply address only when `OUTREACH_INBOUND_DOMAIN` is set; `outreach-inbound` (Resend signature-verified, idempotent) stores replies in `outreach_replies` (team-only) — why: replies are visible in the app and matched to the offer and sender.
- Outreach replies open one `customer_tickets` row (unique `outreach_reply_id`) only when linked to an offer or from a known client and not bulk mail (`ticketSkipReason`) — why: no lead is missed, newsletters don't flood tickets.
- Outreach reply follow-ups are sent only from the admin-edited `outreach_reply_template` (never AI text), at most once per offer, skipping auto-replies/opt-outs (`followUpSkipReason`) — why: automatic emails must stay team-approved and never loop.
- Replies and Customer Tickets show client details by matching email to `clients` via `useClientsByEmail` + `matchClientByEmail` (`src/lib/clientMatch.ts`); profile lives at `/clients/:id` — why: one email-match rule, no duplicated client data.
- Team answers to outreach replies go only through `outreach-reply-send` (team JWT, human-written, opt-out checked, logged in `outreach_messages`, same layout via `renderOutreachHtml`) — why: two-way client email stays reviewed and traceable.
- Client conversation history is built read-only from existing email logs via `buildClientTimeline` (`src/lib/clientTimeline.ts`), never a copied email table — why: one source of truth per email.
